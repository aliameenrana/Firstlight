Firstlight Alarm Clock Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Data collection

Firstlight does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Alarms and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Advertising and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. If you like the app, a star on GitHub is appreciated.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data from children is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions can be filed as an issue at github.com/aliameenrana/Firstlight/issues.
